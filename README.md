# Portfolio

My portfolio site, live at https://oluwatimilehin23.github.io/

Everything is in `index.html`. To add a project, copy the `<article class="project">` block, then change its title, text, links and preview.

## Projects

- [Coca-Cola 3D](https://oluwatimilehin23.github.io/coca-cola/): a Coca-Cola fan-concept page with a 3D can and glass bottle, flavor panels, a shop cart and a contact form ([code](https://github.com/oluwatimilehin23/calculator))
- [EMMY LOGISTIC](https://oluwatimilehin23.github.io/emmy-logistic/): a freight and delivery site from Ado Ekiti, Nigeria with an instant quote calculator, shop, and shipment tracker ([code](https://github.com/oluwatimilehin23/emmy-logistic))
- [Ata & Ember](https://oluwatimilehin23.github.io/ata-ember/): a Nigerian grill kitchen site with a photo menu of real Nigerian dishes and table booking on a 3D floor plan ([code](https://github.com/oluwatimilehin23/ata-ember))
- [Emmy Fashion Store](https://oluwatimilehin23.github.io/emmy-fashion-store/): a fashion store with a scroll-driven 3D handbag and 360° 3D views for every product ([code](https://github.com/oluwatimilehin23/emmy-fashion-store))
