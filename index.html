fizz Coca-Cola 3D

Coca-Cola 3D

A fan-concept landing page for Coca-Cola. A 3D can and glass bottle tilt toward your cursor and spin into each other when you switch products. It's not affiliated with The Coca-Cola Company.

  • A Classic can, a Zero Sugar can and a glass contour bottle, built as 3D models in the browser
  • Switching products spins the model with a motion blur and sends the cherries flying
  • Slide-in panels for flavors, tasting notes, recycling, reviews, a shop cart and a contact form
  • Works on phones too, with a menu button and touch to tilt

Ata & Ember

A website for a made-up Nigerian grill kitchen, with a photo menu of real Nigerian dishes and table booking on a 3D model of the dining room.

  • 18 dishes, from suya and asun to egusi, efo riro and zobo, with photos, prices and allergens
  • A scroll-driven hero where signature dishes swap in on a glowing plate
  • A 3D dining room you can turn, showing which tables are free for your date, time and party size
  • A booking confirmation with a reference and an add-to-calendar file

Emmy Fashion Store

A one-page fashion store for Emmy, built around a 3D handbag that repaints itself as you scroll, so the whole page changes colour with it.

  • A 3D Top Handle bag that sweeps through six colours on scroll
  • 15 bags, shoes, clothes and accessories, each one you can turn 360° in 3D
  • Quick view with colour and size pickers, and a cart that adds it all up
  • Built for phones too: drag to turn, pinch to zoom, tap for details
