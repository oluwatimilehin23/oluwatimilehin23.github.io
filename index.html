Emmy Fashion Store

A one-page fashion store for Emmy, built around a 3D handbag that repaints itself as you scroll, so the whole page changes colour with it.

  • A 3D Top Handle bag that sweeps through six colours on scroll
  • 15 bags, shoes, clothes and accessories, each one you can turn 360° in 3D
  • Quick view with colour and size pickers, and a cart that adds it all up
  • Built for phones too: drag to turn, pinch to zoom, tap for details
